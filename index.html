About the conference

A half-day virtual event that brings employers, companies, recruiters, remote work specialists and job seekers together, to help people earn from the digital economy and find global relocation opportunities.

What you will gain

  • Find remote job opportunities
  • Turn your skills into global careers
  • Explore international opportunities
  • Discover relocation pathways

What happens on the day

  • Keynote on the future of remote work
  • Employer showcase of open roles and internships
  • Workshops on CVs, LinkedIn, interviews and avoiding recruitment scams
  • Virtual career booths and a panel with Q&A

Open to anyone interested in remote work and global opportunities.

About us

Trained graduates, a wide community network and professional recruitment expertise, working together to connect skilled people with meaningful work.

NIME Community

A non-governmental organisation of Nigerians in the Middle East and North Africa, dedicated to collaboration, support and development among its members.

Global Skill Acquisition Program (GSAP)

Trains people in practical, job-ready skills, including EdTech, product design and management, and digital and enterprise skills, and connects them to certification, internships and employment.

Rolestairs Consulting and Recruiting Agency

A consulting and recruitment firm that connects employers with qualified candidates and supports job seekers with matching, CV reviews and interview preparation.
